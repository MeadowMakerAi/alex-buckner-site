Profile · Brooklyn, New York

Alex Buckner

I started in kitchens. These days my work can start with a camera, a packaging proof or a machine layout. The part I like best is following a decision far enough to see what it changes for the next person.

Food & farms

In 2012, John Murphy and I opened Local 215, a Philadelphia food truck and catering company. Greenflash Farm in Hopewell, New Jersey, raised pigs and grew produce for our menus. What came off the farm shaped what I could put on the truck.

Local 215 in Edible Philly

Brands & production

At CG Operations in Cottage Grove, Oregon, I worked in hemp processing and distribution. At Meadow Maker, I work on product and brand decisions together. Choosing a package changes the room I have for an image, the equipment needed to fill it and the work someone has to do by hand.

I work with a Mamiya 7. Photography has a place here both as a practice of its own and as part of the products I make.

Systems & AI

I write specifications and direct AI coding agents to build software for questions I face as an operator. FM Command keeps decisions tied to their sources. The engineering models let me change an assumption and inspect the consequences before committing floor space or capital.

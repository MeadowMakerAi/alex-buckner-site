I've run small businesses my whole working life.

I think in systems. To me a business is a set of connected parts, and the work is designing the parts and the connections between them.

I started in kitchens. I cooked professionally through my twenties and ran Local 215, a catering company and food truck. Kitchens taught me production: timing, yield, consistency, and how a small team gets a lot out the door.

With my partner, I built a hemp manufacturing company to seven-figure revenue. We launched our own brand, which was well received across the country, and we designed and made dozens of products for larger brands.

Today I run three brands in New York's regulated cannabis market, and I'm designing the facility that will produce them.

Along the way I learned to build with AI. I'm not a software engineer. I design the system, write the specifications, and direct AI coding agents to build it. Then I hold the result to written tests. That is how FM Command, the operating system for my businesses, got built.

Long term, I care about local food, regenerative farming, and small companies that own more of their own supply chain.

The greenhouse model

Changing a grow light changes the heat a greenhouse has to shed. I built a simulator with an AI coding agent so I could follow that choice through a full day and across the seasons.

Start with the light

The model opens on a greenhouse in Montgomery, New York. The site, dimensions, fixtures and crop schedule are inputs you can change.

Inside the model

Daylight
Estimate how much sunlight reaches the canopy each month.
Fixtures
Size the supplemental lighting to close the gap to a crop target.
Climate
Trace the resulting power and heat through cooling, ventilation and humidity estimates.

A design decision

Two fixtures can meet the same light target and place different demands on the electrical service and the cooling system. I use the model to compare those cases while holding the site and crop assumptions steady.

These estimates help me choose what to investigate. A capital decision still needs site measurements and engineering review.
